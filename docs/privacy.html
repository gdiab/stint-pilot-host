This Privacy Policy describes how George Diab / Stint Pilot (“Stint,” “we,” “us”) collects and uses information for the personal productivity web and optional SMS pilot at https://stint-pilot.onrender.com.

Who we are

Stint Pilot is operated by George Diab as a sole proprietor. It is a limited personal productivity pilot, not a separate registered company and not a marketing or affiliate lead-generation service.

SMS is optional

Agreeing to receive SMS is voluntary and is not required to use Stint Pilot, complete website setup, or access the service. Messaging consent is collected only if you separately opt in on /onboarding/setup. You may decline SMS and still use the website pilot.

Information we collect

  • Mobile phone number only if you opt in to SMS, plus SMS content you send or receive for verification, session logging, help/opt-out, and optional calendar confirmations.
  • Messaging consent / opt-in records from the optional website consent checkbox on /onboarding/setup (timestamp and agreement text), when you choose to opt in.
  • Onboarding / website session data such as opaque session identifiers and timestamps needed to complete setup (with or without SMS).
  • Google Calendar data only if you connect Google, limited to the scopes you authorize for a designated synthetic calendar.
  • Technical logs such as request times, SMS delivery status from our SMS provider (when SMS is used), and error diagnostics.

How we use information for the messaging program

  • If you opted in: to send transactional Stint Pilot SMS — verification codes, work-session logging confirmations, and optional Google Calendar block confirmations.
  • To honor HELP and STOP requests and maintain an opt-out list.
  • To operate and secure the pilot website and tear it down when the pilot ends.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

How we share information

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We use service processors only as needed to run the pilot:

  • Twilio — SMS delivery and webhook transport (only if you use SMS).
  • Google — Calendar API if you connect Google.
  • Render — application hosting and disk storage.

These processors are not given your number or consent for their own marketing. We do not sell personal information. We do not use pilot SMS or calendar content for advertising.

Retention
