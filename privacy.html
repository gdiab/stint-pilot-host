Stint Pilot Privacy Policy

Effective: September 20, 2026 · Operator: George Diab (sole proprietor) · Contact: george.diab@gmail.com · +1 702-858-4082

This Privacy Policy describes how the temporary Stint Pilot service (“Stint,” “we,” “us”) collects and uses information when you use our SMS and web onboarding for the personal productivity pilot hosted at https://stint-pilot.onrender.com.

Who we are

Stint Pilot is operated by George Diab as a sole proprietor for a limited pilot. It is not a separate registered company.

Information we collect

  • Phone number you use to text the Stint Twilio number, and SMS message content you send or receive for commands, setup, and verification.
  • Onboarding session data such as opaque session credentials, verification codes, and timestamps needed to complete setup.
  • Google Calendar data only if you connect Google during onboarding, limited to the scopes you authorize (typically free/busy and event create/update/delete on a designated synthetic calendar). We do not request access to your full calendar list beyond what the authorized scopes and allowlist require.
  • Technical logs such as request times, delivery status from Twilio, and error diagnostics. We aim to keep provider tokens and secrets out of ordinary logs and browser output.

How we use information

  • To operate the pilot: receive SMS commands, send replies and verification codes, complete browser setup, and create or update calendar blocks you request.
  • To secure the service (session cookies, signature checks, abuse prevention).
  • To tear down the pilot and delete pilot data when the pilot ends, subject to any short retention needed for debugging or legal requirements.

How we share information

We use processors that must handle data to run the pilot:

  • Twilio — SMS delivery and webhook transport.
  • Google — Calendar API if you connect Google.
  • Render — application hosting and disk storage for the pilot.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use pilot SMS or calendar content for advertising.

Retention

Pilot data is intended to live only for the short pilot window (on the order of days to a few weeks). Credential material and the pilot database are deleted as part of end-of-pilot teardown, typically within 24 hours of close for sensitive credential/database data and within 30 days for the service itself, unless a provider legally requires longer retention (in which case the operator records the retained object).
